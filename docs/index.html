SLOOP — HOW TO PLAY

SLOOP is a four-track groovebox: three synth tracks and a drum track, 64-step sequencer, songs, punch-in effects. The golden rule: hold a function button and the 16 white keys and four knobs change job — the screen always shows what they do. Tap the same button instead to open its pages. Hold a layer button and tap HOME to lock the layer open.

FIRST STEPS

  • Play the keys — the selected track's sound plays (track names on the home screen). Turn ALGORITHM to select a track 1–4, PRESETS to change its sound, SELECT to move between pages.
  • PLAY starts the loop. REC while playing records what you play on top (overdub), with your timing preserved. Hold REC ~2 s to clear the track; EDIT + OCT−/OCT+ is undo/redo.
  • On an empty project, tap REC first: the REC screen offers free (no click — your playing sets the tempo) or tempo recording, 1/2/4 bars, and an optional count-in.

DRUMS

Track 4: 16 drum sounds on the white keys, kick to cowbell; a black key doubles its left neighbour for fast rolls. Hold OCT−/OCT+ while playing for ghost/hard hits. 37 kits (PRESETS knob): a sampled acoustic kit and 32 synthesized styles — 808, 909, trap, house, jungle, chiptune and more.

SOUNDS

Nine engines (analog, 4-op FM, phase distortion, trio, organ, voice, granular, lo-fi chip, sampler), 68 level-matched factory sounds browsed by kind, 32 user slots. SCL one-key chords: TRIAD, 7TH, 9TH, SUS4, POWER. Envelope/LFO pages under ENV and LFO.

SONGS

Save up to four sections A–D (SAVE + keys 5–8), launch them live on the next bar (SAVE + keys 1–4), record the arrangement as you play it (SAVE + key 14). In song mode, PLAY runs the whole chain.

FELUCCA — HOW TO PLAY

Felucca is a multi-engine synthesizer: four tracks, one synth part each, thirteen engines, a 64-step sequencer with chords, chance and motion recording, and a modulation matrix. Navigation: tap a page button for its page, tap again for the next; HOME returns home. On action pages and dialogs, OCT+ confirms, OCT− goes back.

FIRST STEPS

  • ALGORITHM selects the track (1–4) on every page; PRESETS browses sounds; SELECT changes tempo on home (and pages elsewhere); KNOB 1–4 edit the four cards on screen.
  • PLAY starts, REC records live with overdub — REC works on every page.
  • Save a sound: stop, tap SAVE, pick a slot with KNOB 1, then OCT+ twice (name it with the keys).

QUICK LAYERS

  • Hold FX — punch-in effects: repeat, reverse, filter sweeps, tape stop, freeze, a harmonizer (OCT UP/DN with shimmer); mutes on the black keys. MENU > FX LATCH makes them toggle.
  • Hold GLO — the mixer: levels, mutes, global settings.
  • Hold SCL — key and scale: 16 scales, white-key mode, one-finger chord keys (in-key triads/sevenths or fixed shapes, with voicings).
  • Hold EDIT — editing shortcuts on keys and knobs.
  • Double-tap FX, GLO, SCL or EDIT to keep the layer open without holding; tap again to close.
  • Hold SAVE — one-step undo. Hold SEQ — the song (chain patterns A–D). Hold HOME — the menu.

SEQUENCER

64 steps per track with chords, ties, accent, slide and per-step chance; a piano roll view; a drum grid (white keys = steps, black keys = lanes); motion recording of knob moves — a motion icon marks the cards it drives; divisions from 4 bars to 1/32. Loading a sound never touches your patterns.

THE ENGINES

ANALOG (two osc + noise, resonant LP) · FM6 (6-operator FM, 32 algorithms, Dexed-based) · PHASE (phase distortion) · LOFI (chiptune with 4-bit wave RAM) · SAMPLE · VOICE (formant vowels) · TRIO (3 osc, ring mod, sync) · WHEEL (tonewheel organ with rotary) · GRAIN (granular) · PHYS (modal resonators, strings, membranes) · NOISE · DRUM. Per track: distortion and slicer; sends to chorus, delay and reverb (ROOM or SPRING); a modulation matrix with 4 slots per track.

THE MENU (HOLD HOME)

PRESETS moves through the rows, KNOB 1 or OCT+ changes the value, hold HOME again to leave. Highlights: COLOR (ten palettes incl. PAPER, NIGHT, HI-CON), STYLE (FLAT/LINE), LARGE (tall knob cards), LEDS, KNOB ACCEL, FX LATCH, BPM LOCK.

IN THIS EMULATOR

  • Hold means click-and-hold (or touch-and-hold) a button — or hold its computer key (1–0 for the function buttons, see the line below).
  • Knobs turn by dragging vertically or scrolling; on a rotated phone, drag along the device's own up — toward its knobs.
  • Your work autosaves to this browser per firmware: projects, settings and user presets survive reloads and firmware switches. The first save happens ~20 s after power-on at the earliest (the firmware's own autosave pacing).
  • Not available here: the USB web editor, sample upload, USB audio, firmware install — and avoid MENU > HARDWARE CALIBRATION (it waits on the real key matrix).
